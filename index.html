Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Area Tool Max ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information.

1. Data Collection & Storage

Area Tool Max does not collect, track, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • No accounts or sign-ups are required.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising identifiers.

2. 3D Model Processing

All 3D files (USDZ, OBJ, STL, etc.) opened in the app are processed entirely on your device. Your 3D models, geometry calculations, and exports are never uploaded to any external server or cloud service.

3. In-App Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed directly through Apple's App Store / StoreKit. We do not have access to your payment details, billing address, or credit card information.
